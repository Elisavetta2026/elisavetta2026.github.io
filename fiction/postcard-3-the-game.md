She cheats. I let her think I don't notice.

We play chess on the small table by the window, evening light coming in sideways, and she's leaning so far forward I can count her eyelashes. Tongue poking out. Hand hovering over the bishop like it's a grenade. She's three moves from checkmate and she doesn't know it, because she's been watching my knight and not my queen, which is — honestly — the most Elise thing that's ever happened at a chessboard.

Ginger sits on the edge of the table and knocks a piece off every four moves. We've stopped correcting him. He's a third player with no strategy and no remorse.

I let her take my rook. She grins. I push my glasses up. The queen is already where she needs to be.

Three moves.
